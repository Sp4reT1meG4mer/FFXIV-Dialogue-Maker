# Credits and rights · FFXIV Dialogue Maker Community Edition v1.0

## 1. Original dialogue-maker inspiration

**FFXIV Dialogue Maker** by **Sebastian Scaini** and **F'ortuna Favore**:

- Original tool: https://sebastianscaini.itch.io/ffxiv-dialogue-overlay-maker
- Original source link (from the tool's project page): https://github.com/sebastianscaini/FFXIV-Dialogue-Maker
- Its itch.io listing marks the original project/assets **Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This Community Edition is an **independent HTML/CSS/JavaScript reimplementation**, developed to provide live preview, separate draggable screenshot and dialogue layers, flexible frame size, and resolution-aware PNG export. It is **not an official new version** by the original creators.

## 2. Source of the five modern dialogue-frame styles

The following artwork was extracted from the *separate*, user-provided **`speech 1.psd`**, identifiable as **Dialogue Template for Screenshots** on XIV Mod Archive:

- PSD template page: https://www.xivmodarchive.com/modid/37755
- Template / Photoshop compilation: **milksnout** (the listing credits the creator under this name, and refers to contact `soft#8888`)
- The template author's own contributor note says **Clawbriar** on **The Spriters Resource** supplied the original sprite files.
- Asset categories: Talk, Shout, Dragon, Deity and Write dialogue backgrounds, matching continuation markers and wide name shadows.

**Important:** A public PSD download is *not* by itself a license to copy or redistribute the extracted sprite artwork. Neither the template listing nor the original dialogue maker's CC0 statement establishes unrestricted redistribution rights for these third-party PNG assets. This archive does **not** grant a license to assets it does not own.
